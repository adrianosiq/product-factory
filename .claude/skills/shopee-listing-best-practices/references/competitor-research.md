# Competitor research — analyse without copying

last_reviewed: 2026-08-28
classification: INTERNAL · SHARED_CORE_CANDIDATE (evidence discipline)

## What competitor Shopee listings may inform

- **vocabulary** — the terms real buyers and sellers use for this product,
- **positioning** — how the product is framed commercially,
- **buyer expectations** — what shoppers in this category look for,
- **category conventions** — image style, attribute fill, title shape.

## What they NEVER prove

```
competitor listing  ≠ product fact
competitor listing  ≠ Shopee policy
competitor listing  ≠ regulatory approval
competitor listing  ≠ brand authorization
```

- A spec, material, certification or identifier on a competitor's listing is
  **not** evidence about our product. It must be independently confirmed against
  `ProductMaster` evidence (SKILL.md §5).
- A competitor doing something (a title format, a claim, a category choice) is
  **not** evidence Shopee permits it.
- Never copy competitor copy, images or descriptions.

## Use in the workflow

Optional (step 7). Extract patterns and objections; feed vocabulary into title /
attribute wording only where it is also true for our product. Record what was
used in `sources_used`.
