# Review mining — market evidence, not product-fact evidence

last_reviewed: 2026-08-28
classification: INTERNAL · SHARED_CORE_CANDIDATE (evidence discipline)

## What buyer reviews of similar products may reveal

- **objections** — recurring reasons buyers are unhappy,
- **expectations** — what buyers assumed they were getting,
- **confusion** — where the listing / category misleads,
- **perceived quality** — durability, finish, accuracy complaints,
- **packaging issues** — damage, missing parts, wrong item.

## What they are NOT

- A buyer review is **market evidence**, not automatically **factual product
  evidence**. "Reviewers say the strap breaks" is a signal to verify the strap
  material / spec against `ProductMaster` evidence — not a fact to publish.
- Reviews are **not** Shopee policy and **not** compliance evidence.

## Use in the workflow

Optional (step 7). Turn objections into return-prevention checks
(`references/return-prevention.md`) and into questions for `ProductMaster`
evidence. Record what was used in `sources_used`.
